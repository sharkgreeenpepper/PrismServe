# Findings

## Research Findings

### Direction 1 — tree-aware multi-GPU placement

- **Status:** `claim_supported: no` for the intended claim that tree/search completion information adds a stable p95 or SLO-goodput benefit beyond common queue/KV-cost routing.
- **Judgment:** A successful GPT-6-Astra same-family review on 2026-10-01 supersedes the 2026-09-30 `REVIEW_UNAVAILABLE` attempt. The judgment is provisional; confidence is high that the current evidence does not support the claim, but low about the true effect or a substantially different method. No experiment-integrity audit was available.
- **What was tested:** Four policies (local-only, least-loaded, fixed-assumption kv-cost, and sibling-lookahead) each ran once on the same 64 frozen prompts from eight synthetic GSM8K trees using two TP=2 vLLM replicas. All had 7/8 majority exact match. Local-only p50/p95 was 124.02/196.26 s; least-loaded 146.34/191.76 s; kv-cost 145.34/207.27 s; sibling-lookahead 147.35/204.39 s. With only eight trees, nearest-rank p95 is the maximum. The least-loaded p95 difference is not stable evidence because its p50 was 18% slower; neither tree-aware policy beat local-only p95. The structural simulator likewise found no stable incremental advantage from sibling-aware oracle routing over kv-cost.
- **Interpretation:** The pilot shows no demonstrated advantage from the tested tree-aware policies. It does not establish that all tree-aware scheduling is ineffective. Accuracy equality on eight questions is descriptive, not a quality-equivalence result. Frozen descendants reuse local-only ancestor outputs; this is not a live dynamic search comparison. The queue/KV heuristic was simplified, did not transfer KV, and was not an independent MemServe reproduction.
- **Constraints for another attempt:** First inspect node-level work, cap/finish outcomes, queue waits, and critical paths. Archive a direction-1 claim contract (the current `idea-stage/IDEA_REPORT.md` now covers direction 3). Then run a bounded, randomized enabled-versus-disabled test of calibrated topology/remaining-work terms under controlled branch imbalance and queue pressure. Continue to real traces/live searches only if the incremental effect replicates. Use deadline goodput for an SLO claim and actual transfer/recomputation measurements for any KV-transfer claim. If the bounded mechanism test stays negative, stop extending the current heuristic and revise or pivot the hypothesis.
- **Evidence check:** 14/14 cited values were located in their source files; this confirms existence, not validity. The full reviewer judgment is preserved locally under `.aris/traces/result-to-claim/2026-10-01_run01/` and intentionally excluded from Git.
