### Direction 1 — tree-aware multi-GPU placement

- **Status:** `claim_supported: no` for the intended claim that tree/search completion information adds a stable p95 or SLO-goodput benefit beyond common queue/KV-cost routing.
- **Judgment:** A successful GPT-6-Astra same-family review on 2026-10-01 supersedes the 2026-09-30 `REVIEW_UNAVAILABLE` attempt. The judgment is provisional; confidence is high that the current evidence does not support the claim, but low about the true effect or a substantially different method. No experiment-integrity audit was available.
- **What was tested:** Four policies (local-only, least-loaded, fixed-assumption kv-cost, and sibling-lookahead) each ran once on the same 64 frozen prompts from eight synthetic GSM8K trees using two TP=2 vLLM replicas. All had 7/8 majority exact match. Local-only p50/p95 was 124.02/196.26 s; least-loaded 146.34/191.76 s; kv-cost 145.34/207.27 s; sibling-lookahead 147.35/204.39 s. With only eight trees, nearest-rank p95 is the maximum. The least-loaded p95 difference is not stable evidence because its p50 was 18% slower; neither tree-aware policy beat local-only p95. The structural simulator likewise found no stable incremental advantage from sibling-aware oracle routing over kv-cost.
- **Interpretation:** The pilot shows no demonstrated advantage from the tested tree-aware policies. It does not establish that all tree-aware scheduling is ineffective. Accuracy equality on eight questions is descriptive, not a quality-equivalence result. Frozen descendants reuse local-only ancestor outputs; this is not a live dynamic search comparison. The queue/KV heuristic was simplified, did not transfer KV, and was not an independent MemServe reproduction.
- **Constraints for another attempt:** First inspect node-level work, cap/finish outcomes, queue waits, and critical paths. Archive a direction-1 claim contract (the current `idea-stage/IDEA_REPORT.md` now covers direction 3). Then run a bounded, randomized enabled-versus-disabled test of calibrated topology/remaining-work terms under controlled branch imbalance and queue pressure. Continue to real traces/live searches only if the incremental effect replicates. Use deadline goodput for an SLO claim and actual transfer/recomputation measurements for any KV-transfer claim. If the bounded mechanism test stays negative, stop extending the current heuristic and revise or pivot the hypothesis.
- **Evidence check:** 14/14 cited values were located in their source files; this confirms existence, not validity. The full reviewer judgment is preserved locally under `.aris/traces/result-to-claim/2026-10-01_run01/` and intentionally excluded from Git.

### Direction 1 — B2 routing replication at concurrency 4 and 8 (2026-10-04)

- **Status:** `claim_supported: no` for a stable incremental latency benefit from the tested tree-aware B2 heuristic over flat queue/KV routing. A same-family GPT-6-Astra review (user-requested light reasoning) returned `no`, confidence high that the supplied evidence does not support the claim; acceptance remains provisional.
- **What was tested:** The same frozen 16-question GSM8K cohort was run at client concurrency 4 and 8, with B2-flat and B2-tree policies, for two runs per cell. Across the four paired comparisons, median tree speedups for B2-tree were +0.10%, +1.78%, −0.78%, and +0.63%; the sign and magnitude varied across repeat and concurrency. All four run-level p95 maxima were numerically lower for B2-tree by 0.22–2.62%, but each p95 is simply the maximum among 16 trees. All eight runs recorded 16/16 majority-correct trees; this is descriptive cohort accuracy only.
- **Mechanism evidence:** Concurrency 8 again reduced client semaphore waiting while increasing vLLM engine-queue waiting, with decode time similar. The data is consistent with shifting the queue boundary into vLLM, but does not establish higher throughput. An earlier factual event replay matched request timing at 1–10 ms grouping windows; at 20 ms B2-flat replay p50 error was 8.75 s and maximum tree error 18.01 s. This is conditional factual replay, not alternate-routing validation.
- **Limits and decision:** Two runs per cell and 16 shared-cohort trees do not estimate run-to-run variability or establish SLO, tail, or quality non-inferiority. The earlier mechanism screen had approximately 10.3% regressions in both blocks. Stop expanding the current heuristic unchanged. The follow-up diagnosis below narrows attention to broad trees 1 and 13; use that to formulate a concrete revised mechanism before considering any new run, with a preregistered practical effect and stopping rule.
- **Integrity and budget:** 61/61 primary-analysis and 64/64 follow-up diagnostic values passed deterministic evidence-existence checking; this is not semantic validation. No independent experiment-integrity audit was available. Cumulative GPU use is 476.487/500 GPU-min; the remaining 23.513 includes the 20 GPU-min cleanup reserve, so no further run was started.
- **Evidence:** See `idea-stage/pilots/direction1/B2_ROUTING_REPLICATION_20261004.md`; full run artifacts remain under `/home/bumi/infra/cache/prismserve-direction1-replication-20261004/attempt2/`. Reviewer trace is local under `.aris/traces/result-to-claim/2026-10-04_run01/` and excluded from Git.

- **Follow-up mechanism diagnosis:** B2-tree changed 54–60 of 128 worker assignments in each comparison, while aggregate generated-token totals varied from −1.22% to +0.24%. Trees 1 and 13 (both broad) regressed in all four comparisons; in repeats, their observed critical-path engine-queue sums rose by 10.5–15.9 s despite similar or lower generated-token totals. Trees 0 (balanced) and 14 (chain) improved in all four. These four individual examples are diagnostic clues only and do not establish a shape-level effect or causality. See `idea-stage/pilots/direction1/B2_ROUTING_MECHANISM_DIAGNOSTICS_20261004.md`.

### Direction 1 — live-tree terminal-budget quality screen (2026-10-04)

- **Status:** claim_supported: no for the intended performance-with-quality claim; B1.6 failed its frozen exact-match screen and B2 was stopped before launch.
- **Reviewer judgment:** GPT-6-Astra at the user's requested light effort (low) returned no with high confidence that the supplied results do not support the claim. Review is same-family; acceptance is provisional. High confidence concerns the evidence-to-claim judgment, not the general effectiveness of tree-aware scheduling.
- **What was tested:** B1.5's first strict-schema quality screen had 3/16 answer coverage and 2/16 exact match versus direct 6/16. B1.6 then reserved terminal nodes on a different fresh 16-question cohort. Direct scored 8/16 exact match; live tree returned numeric answers for 16/16 and scored 5/16. The paired exact-match drop was 3, exceeding the allowed 2. Twenty-three forced-final nodes were used; there were zero forced-final schema violations and zero depth pruning. One of 100 node outputs was malformed after length truncation, while its tree still returned a numeric answer.
- **Interpretation:** The reserved-final policy repaired answer availability for this cohort but did not pass the accuracy screen. Because B1.5 and B1.6 used different cohorts, their coverage difference is descriptive only. B1 supports only that the signal changed same-snapshot assignments on its observed states. No scheduler baseline or performance comparison was run, so p95, SLO, cache, or causal scheduler benefit remains unsupported. This does not prove tree-aware scheduling is generally ineffective.
- **Integrity and evidence:** No dedicated B1.6 experiment-integrity audit exists, so B1.6 integrity status is unavailable. The prior B1 audit is WARN and does not transfer to B1.6. The deterministic evidence check verified 6/6 cited values, which confirms presence but not validity. Reviewer trace: .aris/traces/result-to-claim/2026-10-04_run03/.
